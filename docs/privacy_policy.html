Privacy Policy – PhrasePal

Last updated: August 2026

PhrasePal is an offline travel phrasebook. Phrases, favorites, stars, and look preferences stay on this device. We do not use a microphone, location, or an account.

Data stored on device

  • Favorite phrases and selected language
  • Star balance and unlocked shop items
  • Theme, background, and card skin

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – optional in-app purchases (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N237.json to read whether billing should stay on. No personal data is sent. Timeouts, network errors, and failed requests keep the last known setting.

Permissions

  • Internet – billing and the config check only

Audio

Playback uses the device text-to-speech engine. PhrasePal does not record audio.

Children's privacy

PhrasePal does not collect personal information and does not require an account.
